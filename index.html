6. HOW ADS ARE CHOSEN AND HOW TO CHANGE THIS

The Game contains two kinds of ads:

• Rewarded ads: shown only when you tap a button (e.g. to double your coins, or to upgrade a skill or outfit). The reward is written on the button; watching is always optional, and the reward is given only if you watch to the end.

• Interstitial ads: shown only when you move on from a level you won, and rarely. New players see none during their first minutes, and nobody sees one when leaving a lost level.

The ads are non-personalized (contextual): they are chosen by the type of app, language and approximate location, not by an interest profile. Because we do not use personalized ads, nobody, including anyone under 18, receives profiling-based targeted advertising. Ad content is limited to a family-friendly level. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings > Privacy; no ads are shown after that. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
